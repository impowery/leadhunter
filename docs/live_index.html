How it works

From scattered vacancies and client requests to a focused shortlist you can act on.

1

Scan

Scans job boards, freelance listings, niche communities, and Telegram channels for both vacancies and project leads.

Interval: 4 hours
2

AI scoring

Each opportunity gets a 0 to 100 fit score against your profile. Every claimed match must quote a verbatim line from the advert, and three gates are checked: direction, constraints (remote and pay) and eligibility.

Evidence, not keyword guesses
3

Application package

For every strong match the bot prepares a package: a written first message, standard answers (rate, notice, timezone, links) and the CV. A form autofill opens the vacancy with the fields already filled and stops before Submit.

Letter · answers · CV
4

Deal tracking

Track what happened after each message: replied, called, skipped, or paid. The final decision always stays with you.

replied → called → paid
